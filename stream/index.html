Live Scoreboard

scoreboard.html

An animated score bug you update in real time from your phone — score, inning, count, outs, and base runners. The overlay and your phone stay in sync over the internet, so you can run it from anywhere.

Open overlay ↗ Open phone control ↗
Set it up (2 sources + your phone)
The control panel uses your existing team admin PIN — the same username & PIN as /admin. Anyone without it can’t touch your score.

1 · Overlay in OBS — add a Browser Source:

  1. URL https://bluesteelbaseball.vercel.app/stream/scoreboard (copy it from the setup box above)
  2. Width 1920 · Height 1080 · FPS 30 (or 60)
  3. Leave it on top of your camera. It sits bottom-left by default — add ?pos=br, tl, or tr to move it, or ?scale=1.3 to size it.
  4. Do not tick “refresh when scene becomes active” here — it should stay live and persistent, not restart.

2 · Control from your phone:

  1. On your phone, open https://bluesteelbaseball.vercel.app/stream/scoreboard-control and sign in once (it remembers you).
  2. Tap Ball / Strike / Foul / Out and the count, outs, and half-innings advance automatically; tap bases and scores directly. Every tap updates the overlay instantly.
  3. Hide overlay tucks the bug away between innings; Show overlay brings it back. Banner shows FINAL / MID / rain delay over the count.

Bookmark the control page on your phone’s home screen for one-tap access during games.

Be Right Back

brb.html

Between-innings hold screen — a spinning baseball and “Middle of the Nth,” pulled live from your scoreboard. Drop it up during breaks, rain delays, or lineup changes.

Open full screen ↗ Preview a delay ↗
Notes & customizing

The inning line follows your live scoreboard automatically. Override anything via the URL:

  1. ?msg=Rain Delay — replaces the big “Be Right Back” headline
  2. ?sub=Grab a hot dog — replaces the “Middle of the…” line
  3. ?inning=7 — pins the inning (skips the live hookup)

Add it in OBS the same way as Starting Soon — a full-screen Browser Source at 1920×1080.

Stinger Wipe

stinger.html

A ~1.2s skull-swipe that fully covers the screen at its midpoint, then reveals — for cutting between cameras/scenes. Transparent before and after.

Open & loop ↗ Play once ↗
How to use it — two ways
Best result — a true Stinger transition needs a video file, so record this once, then hand OBS the clip.

Option A · True stinger transition (seamless scene swap):

  1. Open stinger.html (plays once). Screen-record it to a .webm/.mov with transparency — or just record it on a black background if you don’t need alpha.
  2. OBS → Scene Transitions → + → Stinger → pick the clip.
  3. Set Transition Point to 600 ms (the screen is fully covered there — that’s when OBS swaps the scene).

Option B · No recording (Browser Source overlay):

  1. Add stinger.html as a Browser Source on a top layer of the scene you’re cutting to.
  2. Tick “Refresh browser when scene becomes active” — the wipe fires each time you switch to that scene.

Want a ready-made transparent .webm so you can skip recording? Say the word and I’ll produce one.

Logo Bug / Lower Third

bug.html

A small badge that slides in and sits over live video — brands the corner or names what’s on screen. Transparent background.

Open ↗ Corner + custom text ↗
Positions & custom text

All optional, via the URL:

  1. ?pos= corner: bl (default) br tl tr
  2. ?l1= big line (e.g. Blue Steel)   ?l2= small line (e.g. Top of the 7th)
  3. ?hold=8 auto-slides back out after 8s (leave it off to stay up)

Example: bug.html?pos=br&l1=Now Batting&l2=#12 · Blue Steel&hold=10
